A voxel-based weapon customization mod for Minecraft. Customize each voxels to shape your own weapon to your preference and show your own personalized weapons to your friends. Currently, each voxels will give bonus stats such as Attack Damage/Arrow Damage, Attack Speed/Draw Speed, and Durability.

![Voxel Editor](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/zltG2GkM/images/e0e984e1b9e438d6b93a009c7d3f8fc01348e550.png)

## Features
- Customizable weapons - Shape your own weapon to your preference
- Dynamic weapon stats - Each voxel materials provide different bonus stats
- Export - Save your design as blueprint and share it with other people
- Import - Use other people's blueprint as your design. Regular image files works too!
- Weapon abilities - Reach certain material thresholds to activate unique abilities

## Weapon Abilities

### Forged Sword
- Valor: Increases damage for each nearby enemy. (default)
- Life: Restores 10% of attack damage as health on hit.
- Teleport: When hit by any projectile, instantly teleports near the attacker and dodges the damage instance.
- Frostbite: Applies freezing to the target, slowing their movement and attack speed. Hitting a fully frozen target shatters the ice dealing 10.0 damage.
- Double Attack: Performs a second strike upon hitting an entity.

### Forged Bow
- Auto Reload: Automatically releases fully charged arrows and instantly load the next arrow.
- Sniper: Increases arrow damage and velocity at the cost of longer charging time. Movement will be restricted while charging.
- Singularity: Fires an arrow that create a gravitational vortex on impact, pulling nearby entities. Increases charging time.
- Explosive: Arrow explodes upon hitting an entity. Decreases arrow velocity.
- Burst Fire: Fires additional 2 consecutive arrows. Knockbacks will be ignored. (default)
